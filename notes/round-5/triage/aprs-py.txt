1. Pending ruling F2: a non-finite number in a field the report has no place for (about 90 faults lines per seed)

On a weather report (weather data given, weather: {} on "_" included) you skip every check of a field the report has no place for, so a NaN or infinity in base-91 telemetry (sequence, a value, bits), in altitude_feet on an uncompressed weather report or in a voice frequency is never seen, and the record's other faults decide (value-out-of-range from a latitude, extension-conflict, field-not-allowed, invalid-code, invalid-symbol, value-has-no-form). aprs-rs, aprs-ts, aprs-c and aprs-go give not-a-number; aprs-cs does too for telemetry. The rule text pulls both ways ("a non-finite number is not-a-number wherever it is" against "a field with no place gets none of its checks"), so this is up for ruling. The proposal: not-a-number wherever the number is, a field with no place included (NaN's code is decided by its value alone). If it is accepted:

Fix: before any other data check, look at every number given, at every depth, and refuse not-a-number for NaN or infinity, whether or not the report has a place for the field.

Today: refused not-encodable. Expected: refused not-a-number (the other five).

Under the proposed F3 ruling the comparison also comes after every rule check, which gives the same answer.
