Reviewer findings for aprs-go (verdict: merge after fixes). Fix all defects; take suggestions 5, 6, 7, 8, 9.
D1 (most serious: written bytes decode differently). NaN or infinity in a third-party INNER packet's data compares as absent: nfNum (neutral_form.go:51) turns NaN and Inf into null and put drops the key; inner data skips validate (validate.go skips *Packet). Repro: Encode(ThirdParty{Packet: &Packet{Source: N0CALL, Destination: APZ001, Information: "!5130.00N/00030.00W>", Data: PositionReport{... AltitudeFeet: +Inf}}}) returns `}N0CALL>APZ001:!5130.00N/00030.00W>` which decodes with no altitude. Same with a telemetry analog NaN beside `T#1,,001,...`. Fix: inner data gets every check top-level data gets (not-a-number and the rest), at every depth, AND the comparison must never treat a non-finite number as absent.
D2.
