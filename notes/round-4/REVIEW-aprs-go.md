Ruling: pass-through writers of an existing packet (a decoded one, or one whose public field a caller set) are not encoders and do not check; narrow the CHANGELOG and README wording to "every encoder and builder", and say on those three methods that they write the information field as it stands.

Either refuse in Data() as before (preferred: same refusal code as Build()) or list it under breaking changes.

5. Low.
